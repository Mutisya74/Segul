I was really thinking about by first blog post but all have to say, welcome here, 

Back in high school, because of my upbringing (grew up in church) I had a rather different dressing style from my peers. My style was long, modest, "cover-up" skirts — not exactly common for a high school girl living in suburbia.

For a lot of teens back then, the style was either whatever was trendy, or "short clothes." Of course, this got me unnecessary attention from my schoolmates and classmates, and I eventually got a nickname "church girl" - all this at that age without better training on how to handle a situation like that., made me change my style to fit in with the larger group. 

I remember a 2 really unique skirt I had, which I took to the tailor to shorten. I remember the disappointment on my aunt's face when she saw the new length. I still have that skirt, and I deeply regret ever shortening it. In fact, I'm currently looking for similar skirts to the ones I had before, because I trust they were worthy pieces.

Anyway - is there any regret in me trying to change to fit in? I mean, I don't have a single contact left from those people I was trying to fit in with.

I now question the need to hide my God, just to take on the world's standards - which, for anything, only put me under pressure. And this is the case with many Christians: hiding your Christian life at school, at the workplace, just to fit in.

If at all we are going to put our lamps under a bed, **when will our light so shine?**

**(The world cannot become more like Christ, if the church looks more like the world)**

Any environment, any people, that forces you to hide your God, your faith, your standards just to fit in -walk away. Run, and don't look back.

Selah.
